# Project Overview
This report presents a structured analysis of the Superstore Sales Power BI dashboard. The dashboard provides a comprehensive, single-page view of the company’s retail performance, drawing on transactional order data to surface trends in revenue, profitability, regional performance, and product-level margins. The report explains what each visual in the dashboard measures, what questions it is designed to answer, and what business decisions it should inform.

## Business Questions
- What is our total revenue and profit right now?
- Which product category generates the most sales?
- Are sales and profits growing over time?
- Is discounting hurting our profitability?
- Which regions are most and least profitable?
- Which specific products are losing money?
- Are there geographic clusters of underperformance?

## Key Performance Indicators

# What I Learned
Based on the analytical framework embedded in this dashboard, the following areas warrant priority review

## Discount Policy Review
The scatter chart (Discount vs. Profit) should be used to establish a maximum allowable discount threshold per category. Orders beyond that threshold consistently generate losses.

## Sub-Category Rationalization
Any sub-category showing a negative profit bar should be subject to a formal margin review. If cost reduction is not feasible, pricing adjustments or product discontinuation should be considered.

## Regional Strategy
Regions or states with high sales volume but low profit margins should be audited for local cost drivers — including logistics, local promotions, and returns rates.

## Seasonal Planning
Time-series trends in the line chart should inform inventory and staffing plans for peak periods, ensuring that sales volume increases in those periods are accompanied by disciplined margin management.
